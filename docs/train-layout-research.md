Tehran has older separated-car and newer through-gangway stock. The technical
article distinguishes three variants (two newer body variants). This does not
justify different car counts for every AC/DC variant. Avoid forcing riders to
identify traction technology. The useful passenger choices are urban metro,
Line 5 double-deck and the airport shuttle.

Photos/news confirm that women-only accommodation exists in Mashhad, but the
review did not establish current, line-specific full/half-car boundaries.
Do not invent those positions or mark general cars as men-only. Platform and
train signs remain the guide when the reference is unconfirmed or a count differs.
